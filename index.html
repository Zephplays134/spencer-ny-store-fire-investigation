The Shurfine Food Mart fire
15 N. Main St., Spencer, NY

On Friday, September 11, 2026, fire destroyed Spencer's only full-service grocery store. The building was a total loss. The origin and cause remain under investigation by the Tioga County Fire Investigation Team. This page tracks the public record as it develops.

Emergency response

Staff evacuated safely before the building was fully engulfed, and the parking lot was cleared so apparatus could operate. No injuries were reported.

Candor, Halsey Valley, Lockwood, Tioga Center, Waverly-Barton, Campville, Weltonville, Community Fire & Rescue, West Danby, Danby and Newfield fire departments.

Bangs Ambulance, New York State Electric and Gas (NYSEG), the New York State Department of Transportation, and Austin Excavating.

Community impact

The Shurfine Food Mart, remembered by many locals as the former Big M, was the village's only full-service grocery store and a local employer. With it gone, the Spencer–Van Etten area is without a grocery store within roughly a 15-mile radius; the nearest full grocery options are in Sayre, Owego, Watkins Glen or Ithaca. In a statement, the village board said it would do everything within its ability to support efforts to ensure a grocery store or similar essential business remains part of the village, and encouraged the community to remain resilient and united.

Investigation status

Active — cause not determined. The Tioga County Fire Investigation Team is leading the origin-and-cause investigation. As of the most recent published reports, no cause has been announced and no findings have been released.
