The Shurfine Food Mart fire
15 N. Main St., Spencer, NY

On Friday, September 11, 2026, fire destroyed Spencer's only full-service grocery store. The building was a total loss, demolished the same day. The origin and cause remain under investigation by the Tioga County Fire Investigation Team. This page tracks the public record as it develops.

    03 · What was lost

    The store and its people

    Shurfine carried decades of Spencer history. Most locals knew it by an earlier name: the Big M.

    The store was a local institution, remembered as the original Possingers store, which carried the village's two-digit phone number, 38, and later operated for years as the Big M under the Shurfine name. It employed roughly 20 to 30 people and was the only full-service grocery store in the Spencer–Van Etten area.

    05 · The response

    Emergency response

    Staff evacuated safely before the building was fully engulfed, and the parking lot was cleared so apparatus could operate. No injuries were reported. Crews came from three counties: Tompkins, Tioga and Chemung. Spencer–Van Etten Middle School, just blocks away, was not affected.

    Candor, Halsey Valley, Lockwood, Tioga Center, Waverly-Barton, Campville, Weltonville, Community Fire & Rescue, West Danby, Danby and Newfield fire departments.

    Bangs Ambulance, New York State Electric and Gas (NYSEG), the New York State Department of Transportation, and Austin Excavating.

    06 · After the fire

    Community impact

    Losing the village's only grocery left a gap that reaches every part of daily life.

    With the Shurfine gone, the Spencer–Van Etten area is without a grocery store within roughly a 15-mile radius; the nearest full grocery options are in Sayre, Owego, Watkins Glen or Ithaca, about 25 minutes out. The Dollar General in the village is a 10-minute walk but does not fill the gap: staples like milk and eggs have been selling out frequently. Residents who can't order groceries online, including people in their 80s, are hit hardest. In a statement, the village board said it would do everything within its ability to support efforts to ensure a grocery store or similar essential business remains part of the village, and encouraged the community to remain resilient and united.

    Where the community stepped in

    Mutual Aid Tompkins food pantry

    The pantry's cabinet at 160 N. Main St. was lost with the store. A new food-sharing location opened at the Inspire SV-E Community & Fitness Center, 57 E. Tioga St., closer to where the Shurfine once stood.

    Catholic Charities of Tompkins/Tioga

    Emergency assistance for families affected by the fire, from the region's Catholic Charities office.

    The community food cupboard serving households in the school district.

    07 · The open question

    Investigation status

    Active — cause not determined. The Tioga County Fire Investigation Team is leading the origin-and-cause investigation. No cause or findings had been announced as of the Oct. 10, 2026 community follow-up.
